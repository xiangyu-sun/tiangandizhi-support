# Privacy Policy — TianganDizhi

**Last updated: June 7, 2026**

TianganDizhi is built by Xiangyu Sun (sole developer). This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

---

## What TianganDizhi does with your data

### Location

When you grant location permission, TianganDizhi uses your location solely to fetch accurate sunrise, sunset, moonrise, and moonset times via Apple WeatherKit. Location data is not stored, logged, or sent to any server operated by the developer. You can deny location permission at any time; the app will fall back to approximate times.

### Weather

Weather and astronomical data is fetched from Apple WeatherKit using Apple's framework. The developer has no access to your location or weather query data.

### Subscription

TianganDizhi uses Apple's StoreKit framework to manage subscriptions. Purchase and subscription status are handled entirely by Apple. The developer receives only anonymised revenue data from App Store Connect — never your name, email, or payment details.

### Widgets & App Extensions

Widget extensions (iOS, macOS, watchOS) read shared UserDefaults via app groups to display your current settings. No data leaves the device through widgets.

---

## What TianganDizhi does NOT do

- No analytics or crash reporting SDKs
- No advertising networks
- No third-party data sharing
- No tracking of any kind (declared to Apple as "No Tracking")
- No account creation or login required
- No data sent to any server operated by the developer

---

## Data summary

| Data | Where stored | Sent to developer? |
|------|-------------|-------------------|
| Location (if granted) | Used in-memory only | No |
| Weather / astronomical data | Fetched from Apple WeatherKit | No |
| Subscription status | Apple StoreKit | No (Apple handles this) |
| App preferences & settings | UserDefaults (local, shared via app group) | No |

TianganDizhi does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
