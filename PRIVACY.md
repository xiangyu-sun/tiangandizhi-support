# Privacy Policy — TianganDizhi

**Last updated: September 24, 2026**

TianganDizhi is built by Xiangyu Sun (sole developer). This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

---

## What TianganDizhi does with your data

### Location

When you grant location permission, TianganDizhi uses your location solely to fetch accurate sunrise, sunset, moonrise, and moonset times via Apple WeatherKit. Location data is not stored, logged, or sent to any server operated by the developer. You can deny location permission at any time; the app will fall back to approximate times.

### Weather

Weather and astronomical data is fetched from Apple WeatherKit using Apple's framework. The developer has no access to your location or weather query data.

### Subscription

TianganDizhi uses Apple's StoreKit framework to manage subscriptions. Purchase and subscription status are handled entirely by Apple. The developer receives only anonymised revenue data from App Store Connect — never your name, email, or payment details.

### Anonymous usage analytics (iOS only)

Since September 2026, the iOS app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand which features and widgets are used, so development effort goes where it helps most. It collects:

- **App usage events** — for example which tab or reference screen was opened, which widgets are installed and tapped, which settings were changed, and onboarding progress
- **A random app-instance identifier** generated by Firebase — not your name, email, Apple ID, or advertising identifier (IDFA)
- **Basic device information** — device model, iOS version, app version, language, and approximate country/city derived by Google from your IP address

This data is **not linked to your identity** and is **not used for tracking or advertising**. It is processed by Google as a service provider under the [Google Analytics for Firebase terms](https://firebase.google.com/terms/analytics) and retained for at most 14 months.

**You can turn it off at any time:** Settings (設定) → Privacy (隱私) → "Allow anonymous usage statistics" (允許匿名使用統計). When off, nothing is sent.

The Mac and Apple Watch apps, and all widget extensions, contain no analytics.

### Widgets & App Extensions

Widget extensions (iOS, macOS, watchOS) read shared UserDefaults via app groups to display your current settings. No data leaves the device through widgets.

---

## What TianganDizhi does NOT do

- No crash reporting SDKs
- No advertising networks, and no advertising identifier (IDFA)
- No selling or sharing of data with third parties, other than Google processing the anonymous analytics above
- No tracking across other companies' apps or websites (declared to Apple as "No Tracking")
- No account creation or login required
- No data sent to any server operated by the developer

## Data summary

| Data | Where stored | Sent to developer? |
|------|-------------|-------------------|
| Location (if granted) | Used in-memory only | No |
| Weather / astronomical data | Fetched from Apple WeatherKit | No |
| Subscription status | Apple StoreKit | No (Apple handles this) |
| App preferences & settings | UserDefaults (local, shared via app group) | No |
| Anonymous usage events (iOS, can be turned off) | Google Analytics for Firebase | Yes, as aggregated, anonymous statistics |
